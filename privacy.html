Sproutix Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 6, 2026

Sproutix ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information Sproutix collects, how it is used, how it is stored, and the third-party services involved in providing the app.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Account Information

Sproutix uses third-party authentication services, including Firebase Authentication, to allow users to sign in. Depending on the sign-in method, we may receive and store information such as your email address and a unique account identifier.

Plant and Journal Information

Information you create in Sproutix may include plant names, plant-care information, watering information, journal entries, plant progress, identification history, and other information associated with your plants and account.

Photos

Sproutix allows users to capture or select plant photos for features such as plant identification, plant health analysis, progress tracking, and plant journals.

Some photos are uploaded and stored using cloud storage so they can remain associated with your Sproutix account and supported features. Plant Identification history and its associated images may also be stored with your account.

Location Information

With your permission, Sproutix may access your precise location to improve plant identification.

When location assistance is enabled, latitude and longitude may be sent to the Sproutix server and used with PlantNet to determine geographically relevant plant-identification information.

Sproutix does not intentionally retain latitude or longitude in your cloud Plant Identification history. Location access is optional, and Plant Identification can still be used without granting location permission.

Purchase Information

If you purchase Sproutix Pro or another in-app purchase, Apple processes the payment. Sproutix does not receive your full payment-card or bank-account information.

Purchase and entitlement information may be processed and stored by Sproutix and RevenueCat to verify purchases, restore purchases, provide premium features, and maintain your entitlement.

Technical Information

Sproutix and the services used to operate it may process limited technical information necessary to provide, secure, troubleshoot, and maintain the service.

2. HOW WE USE INFORMATION

We use information as necessary to:

  • Create and authenticate Sproutix accounts.
  • Provide and maintain Sproutix features.
  • Save and synchronize plant-related information.
  • Maintain Plant Identification history across supported devices.
  • Provide plant identification and plant-care functionality.
  • Analyze plant photos when users request AI-assisted plant features.
  • Process and verify purchases and premium access.
  • Provide requested plant-care notifications.
  • Respond to support and feedback.
  • Maintain the security and reliability of Sproutix.

3. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

Sproutix relies on third-party service providers to operate certain features. These may include:

  • Google Firebase for authentication, database, storage, and application infrastructure.
  • Amazon Web Services (AWS) for cloud infrastructure and image storage used by certain Sproutix features.
  • PlantNet for plant-identification functionality.
  • OpenAI for certain AI-assisted plant analysis features.
  • RevenueCat for managing and verifying in-app purchase entitlements.
  • Apple for App Store purchases, Sign in with Apple, and platform services.

Information is provided to these services only as necessary to operate the applicable Sproutix feature. These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

4. PLANT IDENTIFICATION AND LOCATION

Plant Identification can use location to improve identification accuracy.

If the user grants location permission, Sproutix obtains latitude and longitude and transmits them to the Sproutix backend. The backend may provide those coordinates to PlantNet for geographic project or regional plant-data selection.

Sproutix does not intentionally persist latitude or longitude in the user's cloud Plant Identification history.

Plant Identification history itself may be stored with the user's Sproutix account so that it can persist across app reinstalls and supported devices.

5. PHOTOS AND IMAGE DATA

Plant photos are used only to provide features requested by the user, such as plant identification, plant health analysis, progress tracking, and journal functionality.

Depending on the feature, images may be transmitted to Sproutix servers or third-party processing services and may be stored in cloud storage when necessary to maintain account-based functionality.

Sproutix does not intentionally rely on embedded photo EXIF location metadata for Plant Identification. Images may be processed or converted before storage or analysis.

6. DATA STORAGE AND SECURITY

Sproutix uses reasonable technical measures designed to protect information.

Account-related information may be stored using Firebase and other cloud infrastructure. Images may be stored using Firebase Storage and/or Amazon Web Services depending on the feature.

Stored images are not intentionally made publicly accessible as general public content. Access may be provided through authenticated or limited-duration mechanisms as necessary for app functionality.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION

Sproutix retains account information and account-backed content for as long as necessary to provide the service, unless the user deletes the applicable information or their account, subject to legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention, and transaction-record requirements.

Users may delete individual Plant Identification history entries. When applicable, Sproutix also removes the associated Sproutix-managed history image when it is no longer required.

Users can delete their Sproutix account through the account-deletion feature available within the app.

Account deletion removes Sproutix account-associated data from Sproutix-controlled storage, including Plant Identification history and associated Sproutix-managed history images, subject to information that may need to be retained for legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention, or transaction-record purposes.

Locally cached Plant Identification history associated with the deleted account is also removed during account deletion.

Signing out is different from deleting an account. Signing out does not delete cloud-backed account information, allowing it to become available again when the user signs back into the same account.

8. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Sproutix is not specifically directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 in violation of applicable law.

9. ADVERTISING AND TRACKING

Sproutix does not currently display third-party advertising.

Sproutix does not use collected information for cross-app or cross-website advertising tracking and does not currently use Apple's advertising identifier for tracking.

10. YOUR CHOICES

Users may:

  • Decline optional location access.
  • Control camera, photo-library, location, and notification permissions through device settings.
  • Choose whether to use features requiring photo uploads or analysis.
  • Delete individual Plant Identification history entries.
  • Delete their Sproutix account using the in-app account-deletion feature.

11. CHANGES TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy as Sproutix changes. When changes are made, the "Last Updated" date on this page will be updated.

12. CONTACT

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, Sproutix, or your information, please use the Contact or Support option available on the Sproutix website.
