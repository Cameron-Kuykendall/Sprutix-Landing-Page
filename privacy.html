PRIVACY POLICY FOR SPROUTIX

Last Updated: October 6, 2026

Sproutix ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information Sproutix collects, how it is used, and the third-party services involved in providing the app.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Account Information

Sproutix uses third-party authentication services to allow users to sign in. This may provide Sproutix with information associated with your account, such as a unique user identifier and, depending on your sign-in method, your email address or basic account information.

Plant and Journal Information

Information you create in Sproutix may include plant names, plant-care information, watering information, journal entries, plant progress, and other information associated with your plants.

Photos

Sproutix allows you to select or capture plant photos for features such as plant identification, plant health analysis, progress tracking, and journal functionality. Photos may be uploaded to our servers and stored using cloud storage services when necessary to provide these features.

Location Information

With your permission, Sproutix may access location information to improve plant identification. Location information may be sent to our server and used with plant-identification services to select geographically relevant plant data. You may decline location access and continue using plant identification without location assistance.

Purchase Information

If you purchase Sproutix Pro or other in-app purchases, purchase and entitlement information is processed through Apple and our purchase-management provider. Sproutix does not receive your full payment-card information.

Usage and Technical Information

Sproutix and the services it relies upon may process limited technical information necessary to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and maintain the app.

2. HOW WE USE INFORMATION

We use information to:

  • Provide and maintain Sproutix.
  • Authenticate users and maintain accounts.
  • Save and synchronize plant-related information.
  • Provide plant identification and plant-care features.
  • Analyze plant photos and provide requested AI-assisted features.
  • Process and verify purchases and premium access.
  • Send plant-care notifications requested through the app.
  • Respond to support or feedback requests.
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the service.

3. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

Sproutix uses third-party service providers to operate certain features. These may include:

  • Firebase / Google services for authentication and application infrastructure.
  • Amazon Web Services (AWS) for cloud infrastructure and image storage.
  • PlantNet for plant-identification functionality.
  • OpenAI for certain AI-assisted plant analysis features.
  • RevenueCat for managing in-app purchase entitlements.
  • Apple for App Store purchases and platform services.

Information is shared with these providers only as necessary to provide the applicable feature or service. Their handling of information may also be governed by their respective privacy policies.

4. PHOTOS AND IMAGE DATA

Plant photos are used to provide features requested by the user. Sproutix does not intentionally use photo metadata such as EXIF location information for identification. Where applicable, images may be processed or converted before storage or analysis.

5. DATA STORAGE AND SECURITY

Sproutix uses reasonable technical measures designed to protect information. Uploaded images and other server-hosted information may be stored using cloud infrastructure. No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. DATA RETENTION AND ACCOUNT DELETION

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide Sproutix and maintain a user's account, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

Users can request deletion of their Sproutix account through the account-deletion feature available within the app. Account deletion is intended to remove information associated with the account from Sproutix's systems, subject to information that may need to be retained for legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention, or transaction-record purposes.

7. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Sproutix is not specifically directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 in violation of applicable law.

8. ADVERTISING AND TRACKING

Sproutix does not currently display third-party advertising and does not use information for cross-app advertising tracking.

9. YOUR CHOICES

You can:

  • Decline optional location access.
  • Control camera, photo-library, location, and notification permissions through your device settings.
  • Choose whether to use features that require uploading or analyzing photos.
  • Delete your Sproutix account using the account-deletion feature in the app.

10. CHANGES TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy as Sproutix changes. When we make changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date on this page.

11. CONTACT

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your information, please use the Contact or Support option available on the Sproutix website.
